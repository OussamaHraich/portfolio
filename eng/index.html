    About me

    Driven by a deep curiosity about how people interact with technology, I am a Senior UI/UX Designer and UX Consultant specializing in bridging the gap between human needs, business objectives, and technical constraints. I thrive in collaborative environments engaging with users, stakeholders, and multidisciplinary teams to demystify complex problem spaces and simplify the relationship between people and interactive digital products.

    UI / UX Design

    Crafting user personas, journey maps, user flows, and wireframes grounded in behavioral research and ergonomic principles, documenting, and maintaining scalable component libraries and design systems that streamline frontend implementation.

    UX Consulting & Agile Strategy

    Building, refining, and prioritizing UX backlogs within Scrum teams and SAFe Agile Release Trains (ARTs), conducting effort estimation, mapping phased UX roadmaps, and aligning design sprints with engineering velocity.
